---
title: Hit and run: report it immediately
tag: Reporting
style: urgent
when:
  type: [hitrun]
---

Call 911 now if you have not. Speed matters here, both for catching the driver and for your own insurance claim, which usually requires prompt reporting.

- [ ] Write down anything you remember about the vehicle now: color, make, body style, partial plate, direction, damage, the driver.
- [ ] Canvass for cameras yourself in the next day or two. Ask businesses and residents to save footage before it overwrites, which can be as little as 72 hours.
- [ ] Ask police for the report number and follow up on the investigation in writing.
