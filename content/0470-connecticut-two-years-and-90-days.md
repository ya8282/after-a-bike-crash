title: Connecticut: two years, and 90 days for a road defect

The general deadline to sue for personal injury in Connecticut is **two years** from the crash.

If your crash was caused by the condition of the road itself, Connecticut's highway defect statutes require written notice to the town or the state commissioner **within 90 days**, and courts apply the content requirements of that notice strictly. This is a situation to put in a lawyer's hands immediately, not to draft yourself.
