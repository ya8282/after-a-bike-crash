---
title: Report the road defect in writing, and date it
tag: Reporting
when:
  type: [road]
---

Photograph the pothole, grate, or plate with something for scale, from several angles, and note the exact location including the nearest address or intersection.

Report it to the city or state agency responsible for that road, in a way that creates a record: 311 in New York City, the municipal public works department elsewhere, or the state DOT for a state highway. Save the confirmation number.

Claims against a government for road conditions are unusually hard and unusually fast to expire. See the deadline section below and talk to a lawyer early.
