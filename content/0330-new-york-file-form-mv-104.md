New York requires a written report to the DMV within 10 days of a crash involving injury, death, or more than $1,000 of property damage. A bicycle crash with a motor vehicle counts, and your bike and gear count toward that damage figure. The form is **MV-104** (the driver's is MV-104A), available at [dmv.ny.gov](https://dmv.ny.gov). Keep a copy of what you send.
