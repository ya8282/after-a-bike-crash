---
title: Suspected broken bone
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [bone]
---

Assume it is broken if there is deformity, grinding, an inability to bear weight or use the limb, or pain directly over the bone rather than the soft tissue.

- [ ] Do not try to straighten it or push anything back into place.
- [ ] Support it in the position you found it. A rolled jacket, a magazine, or a jersey tied as a sling all work.
- [ ] Rings and watches come off now, before swelling makes that a cutting job.
- [ ] Ice over cloth, 20 minutes at a time, never directly on skin.
- [ ] Bone through skin, or a wound over a suspected fracture, means 911 and a sterile cover. Do not wash it out.
- [ ] If fingers or toes beyond the injury are white, blue, cold, or numb, that is urgent: 911.
