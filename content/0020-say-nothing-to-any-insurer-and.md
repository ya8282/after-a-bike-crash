---
title: Say nothing to any insurer, and sign nothing
tag: First
style: urgent
when:
  injury: [fatal]
---

An adjuster may call within days, sometimes before the funeral, and may be kind and sympathetic. Do not give a recorded statement, do not sign a release or a medical authorization, and do not accept any payment. Take their name and claim number, say a lawyer will be in touch, and hang up.

The same goes for anyone asking you to sign for a "quick" payout of funeral costs.
