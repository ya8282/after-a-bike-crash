---
title: Road rash: get the grit out, keep it moist
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [rash]
---

This is a burn, not a scrape, and it scars like one if it is treated casually or left with debris in it.

- [ ] Wash your hands, then rinse the wound under cool running water for several minutes. Volume does the work.
- [ ] Clean around it with mild soap. Get every piece of grit, gravel, and thread out, with tweezers cleaned in alcohol if needed. Anything left behind tattoos permanently and invites infection.
- [ ] Do not use hydrogen peroxide, iodine, or alcohol in the wound. They damage healing tissue.
- [ ] Apply a thin layer of plain petroleum jelly or antibiotic ointment, then a non-stick dressing.
- [ ] **Keep it moist and covered.** Change the dressing daily. A scab is slower and scars worse than a wound kept moist.
- [ ] Photograph it before you cover it, and again every few days.

Get medical help for: grit you cannot remove, a wound deeper than the top layer or wider than your palm, anything over a joint, exposed fat or muscle, or any wound that needs stitches, which generally means within a few hours.

Get a tetanus booster if yours is more than five years old. Watch for spreading redness, increasing pain after day two, pus, red streaks, or fever, and get seen if any appear.
