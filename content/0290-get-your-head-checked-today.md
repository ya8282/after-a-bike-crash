---
title: Get your head checked today
tag: Medical
style: urgent
when:
  injury: [head]
---

Any impact to the head deserves a same-day evaluation, even with a helmet on and even if you never lost consciousness.

**Go to an emergency room now** if you have: worsening headache, repeated vomiting, confusion or trouble finding words, unequal pupils, seizure, clear fluid from the nose or ear, weakness or numbness, or you cannot remember the crash. Anyone on a blood thinner should be seen regardless of how they feel.

Do not ride home. Have someone stay with you tonight.
