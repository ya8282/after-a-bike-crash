---
title: Choosing a lawyer, without being rushed
tag: Legal
when:
  injury: [fatal]
---

Consultations are free and these cases are taken on contingency, so talking to someone early costs nothing. Ask how many cyclist or pedestrian fatality cases they have handled, who will actually work on the file, and how they communicate.

If a lawyer contacts you first, unsolicited, be wary. Ask the advocacy organizations below or a state bar referral service instead.
