New York is a no-fault state, and a cyclist struck by a motor vehicle is generally covered by *that vehicle's* no-fault insurance, no matter who was at fault. It pays medical bills and a portion of lost wages.

You must file the application, form **NF-2**, with the vehicle's insurer **within 30 days of the crash**. This is the deadline cyclists miss most often. Ask the insurer for the form the moment you have the policy number, and send it with proof of delivery.

If the driver is unknown or uninsured, the equivalent route is MVAIC, the Motor Vehicle Accident Indemnification Corporation, which has its own short notice deadline. Contact them right away and ask what they need and by when.
