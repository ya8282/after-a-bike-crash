title: Connecticut: make sure a report exists
tag: Reporting
style: deadline
when:

  type_not: [solo]
---

Connecticut requires reporting crashes involving injury, death, or property damage over $1,000. If police responded, request the report from that department or through the state's crash-report portal. If nobody responded, go to the local police department or the State Police troop that covers the town and file a report in person, as soon as you can.
