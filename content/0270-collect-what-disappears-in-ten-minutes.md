---
title: Collect what disappears in ten minutes
tag: Right now
when:
  timing: [scene]
---

- [ ] Names and phone numbers of every witness. People leave fast; a name with no number is worthless.
- [ ] Wide photos showing the whole scene, the road, signals, sightlines, and lane markings.
- [ ] Close photos of your bike, your helmet, your injuries, and your torn clothing.
- [ ] Look up: note any storefront, doorbell, traffic, or bus cameras pointing at the spot. Footage is often overwritten within days.
- [ ] Write down the time, weather, and light conditions.
