title: New York support and referrals
tag: Support
when:
  state: [NY, NYC]
---

- **Families for Safe Streets New York**: free peer mentoring, monthly support groups including a Spanish-language one, and a post-crash resource guide. **(844) 377-7337**, [support services](https://ny.familiesforsafestreets.org/support-services), [guides in English, Spanish, and Chinese](https://ny.familiesforsafestreets.org/resource-guides).
- **Transportation Alternatives** maintains a list of law firms that specifically handle cyclist and pedestrian cases, and will help connect you: [transalt.org/nyc-bike-resources](https://transalt.org/nyc-bike-resources). We do not endorse particular firms; that list is theirs.
- **New York Bicycling Coalition**'s crash guide, written by bike crash attorneys: [nybc.net](https://nybc.net/nys-bike-law/in-a-crash-2).
- [Bike New York](https://www.bike.nyc) for education and community.
- **211 New York** for free referrals to medical, mental health, and financial assistance near you.
- New York State Bar Association and your county bar run lawyer referral services.
