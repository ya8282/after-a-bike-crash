---
title: Headache, dizziness, or feeling foggy
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [head]
---

Treat this as a concussion until a clinician says otherwise, helmet or not, loss of consciousness or not. Get seen today.

**Emergency room immediately** if the headache keeps worsening, you vomit more than once, you are confused or slurring, one pupil is larger, you have a seizure, there is weakness or numbness, or clear fluid drains from the nose or ear. Anyone on a blood thinner goes regardless.

Otherwise, for the next 24 to 48 hours:

- [ ] Have someone stay with you tonight, or check on you. Sleeping is fine, being alone is not.
- [ ] No riding, no driving, no alcohol, no workouts.
- [ ] Screens and noise in short doses. If a symptom flares, stop and rest, then try again later.
- [ ] For pain, acetaminophen (Tylenol) is generally preferred over ibuprofen or aspirin in the first day or two, since those affect bleeding. Confirm with a clinician, especially about your own medications.
- [ ] Light walking from day two onward helps recovery more than a dark room does.

Do not go back to riding in traffic until symptoms are gone at rest and with exertion. A second impact before the first heals is far more dangerous than the first.
