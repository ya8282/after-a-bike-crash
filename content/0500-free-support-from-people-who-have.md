Families for Safe Streets runs peer mentoring and monthly support groups for people seriously injured in traffic crashes and their caregivers, free, anywhere in the US, plus a guide to dealing with police, insurers, and prosecutors. **(844) 377-7337** · [Post-Crash Resource Guide](https://www.familiesforsafestreets.org/national-resource-guide).

The [Crash Support Network](https://crashsupportnetwork.com/crash-resources/) is an online community of crash survivors.
