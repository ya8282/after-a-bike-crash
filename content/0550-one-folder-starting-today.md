title: One folder, starting today
tag: Records
---

Put all of it in one place, physical or digital:

- [ ] Police report number and the report once you get it
- [ ] Every photo and video, originals, not screenshots
- [ ] Witness names and numbers
- [ ] Driver and insurance details
- [ ] Every medical bill, discharge paper, and prescription receipt
- [ ] Repair estimates and receipts for the bike, helmet, gear, and clothing
- [ ] Missed work: dates, hours, and a note from your employer
- [ ] Your daily symptom log
- [ ] Every letter, email, and claim number from any insurer
