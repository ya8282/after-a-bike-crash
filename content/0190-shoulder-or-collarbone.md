---
title: Shoulder or collarbone
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [shoulder]
---

The collarbone is the classic cycling fracture: you land on the shoulder, and the bone takes it. A visible bump or step in the bone, or pain that spikes when you lift the arm, points that way. A separated AC joint feels similar.

Support the arm in a sling with the elbow bent at about 90 degrees, so the weight comes off the shoulder, and ice over cloth. Get an x-ray; most collarbone fractures heal without surgery, but which ones do depends on the pattern, and that decision is time-sensitive.

If the arm is numb, cold, or weak, or the skin over the bone is tented and pale, go to an emergency room now rather than urgent care.
