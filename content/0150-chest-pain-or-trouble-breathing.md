---
title: Chest pain or trouble breathing
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [chest]
---

Call 911 now if breathing is getting harder, you feel short of breath at rest, your lips or fingertips look blue or grey, or the pain is sharp and stabs with each breath in. A broken rib can puncture a lung, and that can worsen over minutes to hours.

If you are breathing comfortably and it hurts only when you press or twist, it is more likely a bruised or cracked rib. There is no strapping or binding: that causes pneumonia. Instead, hug a pillow against your chest when you cough, and cough deliberately every hour even though it hurts, to keep the lung bases open.

Go back to a doctor for any new fever, a wet cough, or breathing that gets worse over the next few days.
