---
title: Get out of traffic, then stay put
tag: Right now
style: urgent
when:
  timing: [scene]
---

Move yourself and the bike to a sidewalk or shoulder if you can do it safely. If your neck or back hurts, or you cannot feel part of your body, do not move: call 911 and wait.

Do not tell anyone you are fine. Adrenaline hides injuries for hours. Say "I need to be checked out."
