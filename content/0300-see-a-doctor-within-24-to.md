---
title: See a doctor within 24 to 72 hours anyway
tag: Medical
when:
  injury: [moderate, minor]
---

Soft-tissue injuries, concussions, and cracked ribs routinely show up a day or two later. Two reasons to go early: your health, and the gap in treatment that an insurer will later use to argue you were not really hurt.

Tell the provider it was a bicycle crash and describe every sore spot, not just the worst one. Untreated complaints do not exist on paper.
