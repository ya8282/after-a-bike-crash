---
title: Crime victim compensation can cover funeral costs
tag: Practical
when:
  injury: [fatal]
---

Each state runs a fund that can reimburse funeral and burial expenses, counseling, and lost support for families of people killed by a criminal act, which can include a vehicular offense. These are separate from any lawsuit, and they have their own filing deadlines, often measured in months.

- New York: Office of Victim Services, [ovs.ny.gov](https://ovs.ny.gov)
- New Jersey: Victims of Crime Compensation Office, [njvictims.nj.gov](https://njvictims.nj.gov)
- Connecticut: Judicial Branch Office of Victim Services, [jud.ct.gov/crimevictim](https://jud.ct.gov/crimevictim)
