---
title: You feel fine: what to watch for tonight
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [fine]
---

Adrenaline and shock suppress pain for hours, and several serious injuries are quiet at first. Feeling fine at the roadside is normal and is not evidence that you are.

Over the next 48 hours, get seen urgently for any of: a headache that builds, vomiting, confusion or unusual sleepiness, belly pain or a spreading bruise, pain at the tip of a shoulder, breathing that gets harder, blood in urine, or new numbness or weakness.

Ordinary next-day stiffness and bruising is expected. Anything that is worse on day two than day one deserves a phone call.

Get checked within 24 to 72 hours regardless. It protects your health, and a gap in treatment is the first thing an insurer points at to argue you were not really hurt.
