---
title: Connecticut: fault-based, and check your own policy
tag: Insurance
when:
  state: [CT]
  type: [mv, door, hitrun]
---

Connecticut is not a no-fault state, so medical bills generally go through your health insurance first, with a claim against the at-fault driver's liability coverage for the rest.

Check your own auto policy even though you were cycling: medical payments coverage and uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage often extend to you as a pedestrian or cyclist, and that is the main source of recovery in a hit-and-run. Notify your own insurer promptly, since these coverages have their own notice requirements.
