The general deadline to sue for personal injury in New York is **three years** from the crash.

That drops dramatically if a government is involved. Claims against a city, a county, a public authority such as the MTA, or the State typically require a formal **notice of claim within 90 days**, with the lawsuit itself due about a year and 90 days later. Miss the 90 days and the claim is usually gone.

If a city bus, a sanitation truck, a police vehicle, a pothole, or any public agency is part of your story, treat 90 days as your real deadline and call a lawyer this week.
