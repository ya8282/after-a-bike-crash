Families for Safe Streets is run by people who have lost someone to a traffic crash or been seriously injured themselves. They offer free peer mentoring, monthly support groups, and help with the logistics below, to anyone in the United States.

**(844) 377-7337** · [info@familiesforsafestreets.org](mailto:info@familiesforsafestreets.org) · [Post-Crash Resource Guide](https://www.familiesforsafestreets.org/national-resource-guide) (also in [Spanish and Chinese](https://ny.familiesforsafestreets.org/resource-guides)).

Nothing below has to happen today. It is here so that when you are ready, or when someone asks you a question you were not expecting, you know what it is about.
