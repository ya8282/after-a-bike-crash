---
title: Talking to the other side's insurer
tag: Insurance
when:
  type_not: [solo]
---

An adjuster will likely call within days, friendly and quick. Before you engage:

- [ ] Give basic facts only: who, when, where. Nothing about fault or about how you feel.
- [ ] Do not give a recorded statement, and do not sign a medical authorization, until you have talked to a lawyer.
- [ ] Do not accept an early settlement. Once signed, it is final, including for injuries that show up later.
- [ ] Keep every offer, letter, and claim number. Follow up phone calls with a short email summarizing what was said.
- [ ] Stay off social media about the crash and your activities.
