---
title: Neck or back pain: stop moving
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [neck]
---

Do not get up, do not let anyone help you up, and do not take your helmet off. Call 911 and stay where you are, even if you are in an awkward spot, unless there is an immediate danger like fire or traffic that cannot be blocked.

If you must be moved, someone should hold your head in line with your shoulders and move your whole body as one unit, without twisting.

Spinal injuries can feel like a stiff neck for the first hour. The cost of waiting for an ambulance is an hour. The cost of standing up is potentially permanent.
