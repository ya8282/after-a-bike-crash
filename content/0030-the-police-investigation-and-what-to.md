title: The police investigation, and what to ask for

Fatal and critical-injury crashes are handled by a specialist unit rather than the responding patrol officer. In New York City that is the NYPD Collision Investigation Squad; elsewhere it is a state police or county reconstruction unit.

- [ ] Get the name, unit, and direct number of the investigating detective, and ask in writing for updates.
- [ ] Ask whether the case has been referred to the District Attorney or county prosecutor, and ask for that office's victim advocate.
- [ ] Ask what happened to the bicycle, helmet, phone, and clothing. They are usually held as evidence. Do not let them be discarded, and photograph everything before anyone repairs or cleans it.
- [ ] Nearby camera footage is often overwritten within days. Ask investigators to preserve it, and ask nearby businesses yourself.
- [ ] You are entitled to the crash report and, in most cases, the reconstruction file. Ask how to request both.

Investigations often close quietly with no charges. That outcome does not decide your civil claim, which is separate and has its own deadlines.
