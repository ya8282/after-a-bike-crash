---
title: Stop and call 911 if any of these are true
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
---

Whatever else is going on, these mean an ambulance, not a car ride and not a wait-and-see:

- Not fully awake, confused, or cannot remember the crash
- Bleeding that does not slow after several minutes of firm pressure
- Neck or back pain, or numbness, tingling, or weakness anywhere
- Trouble breathing, or chest pain that worsens when breathing in
- A limb that looks bent wrong, or bone visible through skin
- Belly pain, a rigid or swelling abdomen, or pain at the tip of the shoulder
- Repeated vomiting, a seizure, or clear fluid from the nose or ear
- Pregnancy, a bleeding disorder, or any blood thinner, after any real impact

Everything below is first aid, not a diagnosis. It buys time until someone qualified looks at you. When you are unsure, assume the worse thing and call.
