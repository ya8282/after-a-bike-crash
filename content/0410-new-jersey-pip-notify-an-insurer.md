New Jersey personal injury protection can cover a cyclist hit by a motor vehicle. Which policy pays depends on the rules about your own auto insurance and your household's, so do not assume you have nothing to claim just because you were on a bike.

Notify the relevant insurer promptly and ask for the PIP application. Many treatments need pre-authorization, so getting the claim open early protects your bills.

If the driver is unknown or uninsured, ask about the Unsatisfied Claim and Judgment Fund, which has a short notice period, on the order of 90 days.
