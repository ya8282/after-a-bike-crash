---
title: Death certificate, medical examiner, and the estate
tag: Practical
when:
  injury: [fatal]
---

You will need certified copies of the death certificate, and more than you expect: order at least ten. The funeral home usually orders them for you.

The medical examiner or coroner will likely perform an autopsy. You can request the report later; it can matter in a claim.

A wrongful death claim is brought by a personal representative of the estate, not by the family directly, and that appointment happens in Surrogate's Court in New York, Superior Court in New Jersey, or Probate Court in Connecticut. It takes time, which is one reason to talk to a lawyer well before the deadline.
