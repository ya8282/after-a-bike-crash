---
title: Belly pain: this is the one that hides
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [abdo]
---

Handlebars hit the abdomen hard and can injure the spleen, liver, or bowel with almost nothing to see on the outside. Bleeding inside can take hours or occasionally days to declare itself, and people have died going home feeling bruised.

**Emergency room now** for: pain that is worsening rather than settling, a belly that is rigid, swollen, or tender all over, pain at the tip of the left shoulder, blood in urine, vomit, or stool, or any bruise in the shape of the bars or your seat.

If you were hit in the abdomen at speed, get it checked even if it feels manageable. Nothing by mouth on the way, in case a surgeon needs you empty.
