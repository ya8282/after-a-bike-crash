---
title: Teeth, face, or eye
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [face]
---

**A knocked-out adult tooth is a one-hour emergency.** Pick it up by the crown, never the root. If it is dirty, rinse briefly in milk or saline, not water, and do not scrub it. Put it straight back in the socket and bite gently on a cloth. If you cannot, keep it in milk, or in your own saliva against the inside of your cheek if you are alert. Get to a dentist or emergency room immediately. Time in the socket is what decides whether the tooth survives.

Chipped or loosened teeth also need a dentist promptly. Save any fragments in milk.

For the face: pressure for bleeding, ice over cloth for swelling. A nosebleed means leaning forward and pinching the soft part for ten minutes. Numbness in the cheek or lip, double vision, a change in your bite, or an eye socket that feels different means an emergency room.

For the eye: do not rub it and do not remove anything stuck in it. Cover it with something rigid rather than a pad if it is punctured, cover both eyes if you can, and go. Vision loss, flashes, floaters, or blood in the coloured part are all emergencies.
