title: The part people skip
tag: Recovery
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
---

Crashes rattle people. Trouble sleeping, replaying it, flinching at traffic, anger, or dreading the bike is an ordinary response to being hit, not weakness, and it usually eases over a few weeks. If it is not easing, or you are avoiding things you need to do, talk to a doctor or a therapist. Mention the crash: trauma responses are treatable and respond well to early care.

In crisis, call or text **988**.

When you ride again, go short, go somewhere quiet, and go with someone. Have a shop inspect the frame, fork, and wheels first, especially anything carbon, where damage hides.
