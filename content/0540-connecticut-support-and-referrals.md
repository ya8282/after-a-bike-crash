- **Families for Safe Streets** supports crash victims anywhere in the US, free: peer mentoring, support groups, and a [post-crash resource guide](https://www.familiesforsafestreets.org/national-resource-guide). **(844) 377-7337**, [info@familiesforsafestreets.org](mailto:info@familiesforsafestreets.org).
- [Bike Walk Connecticut](https://bikewalkct.org) for advocacy and local connections.
- **211 Connecticut** for referrals to medical, mental health, and financial help.
- Connecticut Bar Association lawyer referral service.
