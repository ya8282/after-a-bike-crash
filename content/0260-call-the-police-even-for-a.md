---
title: Call the police, even for a minor crash
tag: Right now
when:
  timing: [scene]
---

A police report is the single most useful document you can have later, for insurance and for any claim. Officers sometimes tell cyclists a report is not needed. Ask anyway, politely and clearly: "I want a report, and I am injured."

Get the officer's name, badge number, and the report or complaint number before they leave.
