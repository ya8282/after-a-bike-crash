---
title: Heavy bleeding: firm, constant pressure
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [bleed]
---

- [ ] Press hard directly on the wound with a cloth, a jersey, a glove, anything. Firm and continuous beats gentle and fidgety.
- [ ] Hold it for a full ten minutes without lifting to peek. Lifting restarts the clock.
- [ ] If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top. Do not remove the first layer.
- [ ] Raise the limb above the heart if doing so does not cause pain that suggests a fracture.
- [ ] Do not pull out anything embedded in the wound: glass, metal, a branch. Pad around it and leave it for the hospital.

If bleeding from an arm or leg is spurting or will not slow, and you have a tourniquet or a belt and strap, apply it high and tight above the wound, tighten until the bleeding stops, and note the time. That limb needs an emergency room immediately.

Feeling cold, clammy, grey, or suddenly very thirsty is shock. Lie down, stay warm, and call 911.
