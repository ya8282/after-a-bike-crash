---
title: Your own coverage for the bike and gear
tag: Insurance
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
---

Homeowner's or renter's insurance often covers a damaged bicycle, subject to the deductible. Get a written repair estimate or replacement quote from a shop, and hold on to the original purchase receipts if you have them. Photograph the damage before any repair.

Health insurance covers treatment now, and may later assert a lien against any settlement. That is normal. Keep the correspondence.
