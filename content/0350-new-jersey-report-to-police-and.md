New Jersey requires crashes involving injury, death, or apparent property damage over $500 to be reported to police immediately. If no officer investigated at the scene, you must file a written report with the NJ Division of State Police within 10 days. Look for the crash report form at [nj.gov/njsp](https://nj.gov/njsp).

You can request the police report from the responding department, usually after about five business days, for a small fee.
