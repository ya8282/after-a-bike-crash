---
title: New York: wrongful death is two years, not three
tag: Deadlines
style: deadline
when:
  injury: [fatal]
  state: [NY, NYC]
---

New York's wrongful death deadline is **two years from the date of death**, shorter than the three years for personal injury, and it runs from death rather than from the crash. A survival claim for the pain your person suffered before dying has its own timing. An estate representative must be appointed before the case can be filed.

If a city, county, public authority such as the MTA, or the State may be responsible, a formal **notice of claim is due within 90 days**. That is the real deadline. Call a lawyer this week, not this year.
