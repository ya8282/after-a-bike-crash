Traumatic loss is not ordinary grief. Intrusive images, anger at the investigation, guilt over details that were never yours to control, and exhaustion from paperwork are all common, and they can arrive months later. Trauma-informed therapy helps, and grief groups of people who lost someone the same way help in a different way.

Reporters may contact you. You are never obligated to speak to them, and you can ask a friend to field it.

In crisis, call or text **988**. Families for Safe Streets peer mentoring is at **(844) 377-7337**.
