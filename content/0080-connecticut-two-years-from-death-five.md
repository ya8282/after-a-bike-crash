Connecticut's wrongful death deadline is generally **two years from the date of death**, and in no event more than five years from the act that caused it. The claim is brought by the executor or administrator of the estate.

If the road itself was a cause, the highway defect statutes require written notice to the town or the state commissioner **within 90 days**, and courts apply the content requirements strictly. Put that one in a lawyer's hands immediately.
