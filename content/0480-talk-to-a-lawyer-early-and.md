---
title: Talk to a lawyer, early and for free
tag: Legal
when:
  type_not: [solo]
  injury_not: [minor, fatal]
---

Personal injury consultations are free and these lawyers work on contingency, so an early conversation costs you nothing. Early matters: evidence disappears, camera footage overwrites, and the short government deadlines above run whether or not you knew about them.

Look for someone who specifically handles bicycle cases. State and county bar associations run lawyer referral services, and local cycling advocacy organizations keep lists. Ask how many cyclist cases they have handled and who will actually work on yours.
