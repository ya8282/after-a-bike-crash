---
title: A solo crash may still be someone's fault
tag: Legal
when:
  type: [solo]
---

If a road defect, construction plate, debris in a bike lane, a badly maintained path, a defective bike component, or a shop's faulty repair put you down, there may still be a claim. Photograph the cause before it is fixed, report it in writing, and keep the parts. The government deadlines above apply here too.
