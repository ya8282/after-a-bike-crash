---
title: Wrist or hand: ask specifically about the scaphoid
tag: First aid
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [wrist]
---

Landing on an outstretched hand commonly breaks the scaphoid, a small wrist bone. It matters because **the first x-ray is often normal**, people are told they sprained it, and the bone then fails to heal and causes lasting arthritis.

The tell is tenderness in the anatomical snuffbox, the hollow at the base of the thumb when you stick the thumb out. If pressing there hurts, say the word "scaphoid" to the clinician and ask about a splint and a repeat x-ray or scan in 10 to 14 days even if the first film is clean.

Meanwhile: splint the wrist, rings off, ice over cloth, elevate above the heart.
