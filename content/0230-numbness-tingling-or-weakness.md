---
title: Numbness, tingling, or weakness
tag: First aid
style: urgent
when:
  injury_not: [fatal]
  symptom: [numb]
---

New numbness or weakness after a crash is a call-911 symptom, not a wait-until-morning symptom, because it can mean a spinal cord or nerve injury. That is especially true if it affects both sides, a whole limb, or anything below the waist, or if you have neck or back pain with it.

Do not move around while waiting. Note exactly which fingers, toes, or areas are affected, and whether it is changing. That detail genuinely helps whoever sees you.

Long-standing pins and needles in the hands from gripping the bars is a different, benign thing. New since the crash is what matters.
