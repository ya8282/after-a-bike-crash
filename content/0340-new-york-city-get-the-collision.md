---
title: New York City: get the collision report and use 311
tag: Reporting
when:
  state: [NYC]
---

NYPD collision reports can be requested online through the NYPD Collision Report Retrieval System, generally a few days after the crash. Search "NYPD collision report" on [nyc.gov](https://nyc.gov). You will need the date, location, and report number.

Report the street condition, signal, or dangerous intersection to **311** as well. That creates a dated public record of the hazard.
